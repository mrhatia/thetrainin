What does a Learning Strategist do?

Learning strategists help organizations turn business goals into clear, actionable learning plans. The work usually involves:

Working with leaders to understand priorities, success metrics, and desired outcomes
Identifying capability gaps through interviews, data, and observation
Advising on formats and delivery methods that best fit the audience
Creating learning portfolios across onboarding, leadership, systems, and role specific learning
Supporting stakeholders through planning, decision making, and change impacts
Using learner insights and performance data to strengthen programs over time
